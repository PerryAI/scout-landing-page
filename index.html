Questions people ask

Short answers to what most people want to know first.

How does the waitlist work?

You answer a few questions so we know your field and experience. When a spot opens in your field, we email you to verify your background and get you started. Joining is free and there's nothing to upload.

How much can I earn?

Each mission pays between $50 and $500, depending on the work and the time it takes. What you make overall depends on how many missions you take on.

How much time does it take?

Most sessions take 10 to 45 minutes. Some missions run over several days with a session each day. You'll see the time and the number of days before you accept.

What do I need to join?

A license, a degree, or experience in your field that we can verify. Any profession can apply.

How do I get paid?

Through PayPal, within 10 business days of finishing a mission.

Is my information private?

Yes. We never share your name or email, and your answers are anonymized.

Will I see real patient records?

No. Clinical missions use made-up or simulated cases only.

Is this a job?

No. There's no schedule and no commitment. You choose the missions you want.

Ready to join?

It takes about two minutes. Spots are limited, and we'll email you when one opens in your field.
