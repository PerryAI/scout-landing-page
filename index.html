05 · Questions

Questions, answered.

The fastest answer is the 2-minute check.

Is this real, and will I actually get paid?

Yes. Every mission shows its pay before you accept. Work that follows the mission instructions is paid to PayPal within 10 business days.

How much can I earn?

Missions pay $50 to $500 each, depending on the task and how long it takes. What you earn overall depends on how many you choose to do.

How much time does a mission take?

10 to 45 minutes in total. Some missions are done in several short entries spread across the week. You decide how many to take on.

What do I need to join?

A license, a degree, or another background we can verify in your field. There's nothing to upload to apply. You verify after.

How do you verify my expertise?

We confirm your license, your degree, or your experience in your area. It usually takes about 2 business days.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your identity stays private and your answers are anonymized. We never share your name or email.

Will I see real patient records?

No. Clinical missions use fictional or simulated cases only.

Is this a job?

No. There's no schedule or commitment. You take the missions you want.
