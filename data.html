  • You keep ownership

    micro1 licenses your data. The underlying data stays your company's.

  • You set the scope

    Before anything is shared, you agree on what's included, the security standards, and what gets redacted.

  • Personal details are scrubbed

    Sensitive information is removed before any use. Some datasets are rewritten so the workflow stays but the link to real records doesn't.

  • You see samples first

    You review representative samples before your data is used.

  • Locked down, then deleted

    Only a small authorized team can access it, nothing is made public, and it's deleted after the agreed period or when you ask.

Questions people ask

Who runs this program?

micro1, an AI data company that builds training environments and evaluations for AI labs. Winston introduces companies to its data partnership program.

Why would AI labs pay for our data?

Public text is easy to find. Records of how real companies run are not. AI systems need them to learn real work: how teams follow processes, make decisions, and hand work off.

Do we keep ownership of our data?

Yes. micro1 licenses your data, and your company keeps ownership of it.

How is our data anonymized?

Personal and sensitive details are scrubbed before any use. Some datasets are also rewritten, keeping the workflow but not the link to real records. You agree on what gets redacted, and you review samples first.

How much will we be paid?

It depends on how much data you have, how complex and unique it is, and its quality. micro1's published tiers for operating companies start at $100K. Offers for companies that have shut down are set case by case.

Our company has shut down. Can we apply?

Yes, if it had 50 or more employees at its peak, has substantial Slack or Microsoft Teams history, and was based in one of the listed countries. The person applying should have authority over the company's records.

What happens when I click See if you qualify?

You'll go to micro1's data partnership portal and create a company account with your work email. micro1 takes it from there.

How is Winston involved?

Winston introduces companies to micro1. Your application, agreement and payment are all with micro1, and Winston never sees your data.

Ready to apply?

It takes a few minutes. Applying is free, and nothing is shared until you agree on the scope.
