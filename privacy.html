Privacy policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

This page explains what Winston collects when you visit trywinston.app or join the waitlist, and what we do with it. We keep it short and only collect what we need.

What you give us

When you join the waitlist, we save the answers you enter:

  • Your email address and phone number
  • Your field, and what you typed if you chose "Another field"
  • How your background can be verified (license, degree, or other)
  • Your years of experience

What we collect automatically

  • Ad and link details. If you arrive from an ad or a tracked link, we save the campaign tags in that link (such as utm_source) and the click IDs that Meta or Google add (fbclid, gclid), along with the time you signed up.
  • Meta Pixel. The site uses the Meta Pixel, which uses cookies and similar technology. It tells Meta when someone visits the site and when someone joins the waitlist (including the field they chose), so we can see which ads work and show them to the right people. Meta handles this data under its own privacy policy.
  • Site analytics. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages people view and where they came from. It does not use cookies and does not identify you personally.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting provider records standard information such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, to run and secure the site.

How we use it

  • To run the waitlist and contact you by email, phone, or text about your spot, such as when one opens in your field
  • To check whether your background fits the missions we have
  • To measure and improve our ads
  • To keep the site working and secure

We do not send you marketing. We only contact you about your spot on the waitlist and the missions you sign up for. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that help us run the site: Vercel (hosting and site analytics), Neon (our database), and Meta (ad measurement, through the Pixel). We may also share information if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete your information at any time by replying to any email from Winston.
  • You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, which also limits the Meta Pixel.
  • You can control how Meta uses your activity for ads in your Meta ad preferences.

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist answers until you ask us to delete them or we no longer need them to run the waitlist.

Children

Winston is for working professionals. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
